# Community conduct

Make this project a respectful place to ask questions and contribute.

- Discuss code and evidence rather than attacking people.
- Welcome different backgrounds, experience levels, languages, and viewpoints.
- Accept constructive feedback and respect personal boundaries.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, publish private information, or send unwanted sexual content.
- Do not spam or pressure contributors to provide unpaid support on a deadline.

These rules apply to project issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces where someone represents the project. Maintainers may remove content, close discussions, restrict participation, or ban participants in proportion to the conduct. They should explain decisions privately when practical and protect reporters' privacy.

The private conduct reporting address awaits the owner's confirmation. A working route must be listed before opening external participation. Do not publish identifying details about reports. If a complaint involves a maintainer, use an independent platform reporting channel when available.
